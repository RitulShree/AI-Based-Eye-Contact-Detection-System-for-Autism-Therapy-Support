Documentation

How It Works

▼
01

What Is Measured

The system tracks 11 behavioral metrics using your webcam, including eye contact percentage, blink rate, gaze variance, fixation count, and inter-blink interval, during a 60-second session.

02

How the ML Model Works

A Random Forest classifier trained on 92 labeled sessions (46 typical, 46 atypical) analyzes the extracted metrics and classifies gaze behavior. The model achieved 84% accuracy with cross-validation.

03

What Typical Means

Typical gaze behavior indicates eye contact percentage and fixation patterns consistent with normative behavioral data. It does not mean the person has no condition, only that gaze patterns appear within expected range.

04

What Atypical Means

Atypical gaze behavior indicates patterns outside the normative range, including reduced eye contact, irregular fixations, or high gaze variance. This is a behavioral flag, not a diagnosis of autism.

05

System Limitations

This tool is a behavioral screening aid only. Lighting conditions, camera quality, and patient cooperation can affect results. It cannot replace a formal clinical evaluation or standardized diagnostic assessment.

06

Intended Use

Designed for use at hospital entry points as a first-level behavioral screening tool. Results should always be reviewed by a qualified clinician before any action is taken.
