Reception Instructions

Patients complete a 60-second behavioral eye-contact screening at the kiosk. Each completed session generates a token number and is logged in this queue.

  • Monitor newly generated tokens appearing in the table.
  • Call patients based on token order.
  • Check screening result (Typical / Atypical).
  • Guide the patient to the appropriate doctor or specialist.
  • Click Refresh to update the queue.

⚠️ This system is a screening tool only. Final decisions must be made by a qualified clinician.
